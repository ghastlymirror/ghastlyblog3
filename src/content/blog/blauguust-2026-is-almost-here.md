Greetings, it´s been a minute since I last posted. August is coming so you know what that means... Yes, I´m talking about Blaugust.

If you´re new to my blog (in that case thank I hope you will stick around), Blaugust is this community event in the blogging space where us bloggers are encouraged to pick up our websites or whatever online publish paltform of choice, and post during the month of August, preferably during 31 days.

Also it encourages people to interact and be a part of an online blog community and great way for those just starting out or in the fence of dipping their toes in the blog pool before decided is this is something they would want to commit to.

At the end of it you get to have a small badge for bragging rights for your site, and hopefully some writting inspiration. Which in the age of AI generation we´re gonna need more of.

This event was of course originally created by Belghast, owner of [Tales of the Aggronaut](https://aggronaut.com/), whom sadly has recently passed away. I did not know him personally since I only followed his blog and was only casuallu active on his discord, but from what people had to say [about him](https://aywren.com/2026/07/04/in-memory-of-belghast-the-one-true-aggronaut/), he seemed like [a cool dude](https://massivelyop.com/2026/07/03/obituary-mmorpg-blogger-and-community-leader-mark-belghast-temple-has-passed-away/). I do wish I could´ve interact with him more.

In a way the continuation of this event is a way to keep his memory alive. 

Last year I wasn´t as active as I want but I´m gonna try to get more stuff out there next month. To be Honest I´m not the kind of person to plan my post, I usually just wing it.

## So How Do I participate?

Now that I´ve got your attention, you might ask, how do I join in?

Step one, you can [fill this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScouIfF_RGkOPhWbyDjAhGTnxJ0smWfhZEiZRiAeLnvzPuO9g/viewform), you can also join the [Blaugust Discord](https://discord.gg/EVvMnEGvgV).

You can also find more information [on here](https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/07/15/blaugust-2026-is-coming/) and [this post](https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/07/23/blaugust-2026-calendar-weekly-prompts-group-projects/).

Other things to consider, is that it doesnt really matter where you do it, you can blog, on your own hosted site, on Tumblr, [Pillowfort](https://www.pillowfort.social/), [Bearblog](https://bearblog.dev/) even [Dreamwidth](https://www.dreamwidth.org/). Any Platfrom that allows for longform writting will do.

And lastly, don´t feel pressured to have to post every day, the point to encourage people to have fun blogging, you can try post as much as you´re able.

Hopefully I´ll get to read some of what you post, feel free to comment with a link to your blog. Happy writting and see yall In Blaugust!
