## Cabernet

![A young woman with fangs in a victorian dress with a dead rabbit on her arms with blood on her mouth](/images/2026/cabernet.png)

A nice little narrative rpg about a young Doctor in training who gets turned into a vampire and now has to learn how to survive in this new dark world.

I don´t remember if I ever mentioned this game here on my blog. But now that I have managed to put some hours in, I gotta say the story got me hooked. Specially with the characters, I think I managed to help a hedonist vampire with her goals of seeing the sun without frying and freeing someone from their debt through a loophole in a contract. That was fun.

You are given some moral choices that lets you roleplay a either a reluctant vampire trying to hold on to your humanity, or someone who has given in to her worst impulses. Or you can just play as someone who is morally grey. And yes some dialogue choices will only open up depending on your moral meter, or what skills you invested in. (Boy, I missed on on some options because I invested more points on to Logic and History)

I´m gonna forget to mention that like the newly published Dawnwalker it has a time limit. You wake each night, and certain things in the game the clock foward, wether you are doing a quests, reading or doing medical tasks, moving the clock ever so closer to the dawn, forcing to quickly flee to your coffin only to star all over again. And also certain quests need to be done in a specific day.

Meaning I´m probably not gonna see everything in a single playthrough, and if I wish to develop relantionships withc certain character I had to piroritez them over others. And yes its possible to fail quests. On my first playthrough one character ended up dead before I could complete there´s so.

But the game does move on regardless. And it does a pretty of giving the experience of what is like to be a vampire and what kind of moral comprises it would entail. Specially when there have been times where I got really close to starvation and had to well feed on character´s I´ve befriended. 

Well, I go out of my way to feed without killing but still.

Silverlining wandering around as a bat is kinda cool.

## Foolish Mortals

![A young man in a yellow waistcoat with a map on his hand while staring at the viewer, behind him there are two ghost, one of a woman in bridal dress another a man with a suit and tophat](/images/2026/foolishmortals.png)

I had my eye on this one for a while, then i saw it was on sale and kinda cave in along with other titles (RIP My Wallet).

This is because I think I might have been very heavily inspired by the Haunted Mansion Ride from Disney, one of my favorite rides, since I was a kid lately. Or at least as a spritual adaptation. I mean haunted mansion in Louisiana. Mysterious Deaths involving a Bride..

Heck, the title alone is a phrase used by the Ride´s Ghost Host that greet you at the begginning.

oh wait now I remember, David Younger who worked on this game has a background on theme park design, and he also mentioning about conceiving the game as what would be ,life if you could just step out fo the doombuggie from the Haunted Mansion, the same way Ron Gilbert created Monkey island to feel like the [Pirates of the Caribean ride.](https://indiegamesdevel.com/foolish-mortals-interview-with-david-younger/)

After playing it for a couple of hourse or so, I can confirm yeah it gives that vibe, of course rather just spend all your time in the mansion, there is also a town around to explore too, and I´m currently on the first chapter trying to get the ingredients that will allow me to contact the ghosts.

I mean the game has this disney artstyle to it, and there is a strong emphasis on good voice acting, there also some easpects of it that also remind me of the Princess and the Frog movie, but with more darker themes.

So, before I forget to mention, you play as an auditor charged with finding the long lost treasure of the Bellmore mention, and have one night to do it before the claims to it expire. And there´s a mytery surrounding the mansion where a bunch of people went missing during a wedding.

As with a lot of point and  ckick adventures games I´ve been palying over the years, don´t expect for it to innovate on the formula on that much, so its strenght is focused on its story and dialogue. Might get around to write a review in more delaied once I finished it.

##  The Seance of Blake Manor

![A man in victorian style of dress surround by huge stones with celtic symbols, a manor in the background under the full moon](/images/2026/blakemanor.png)

Haven´t started this one yet, but Iam really planning on playing this one once Im done with the ones I´m already playing. It caught my attention because its artstyle reminded me of Mike Mignola, known for his work on Hellboy. Which is my favorite artstyle.

So you´re getting a cool comic artstyle for game that involves the supernatural.

Another reason for me picking this up on sale recently is because its a first person detective game, where you explore around the mansion and interrogate quests, not to mention using Irish mythology and the Victorian Spiritualists movements for inspiration.

Anyway, it´s Halloween in 1897 a group of occultists gather for a Seance in an manor but two days before that, one of the guests, a young woman Miss Deane,goes missing. As a Detective its up to the player to uncover the dark secrets of the house and its residents.

That´s right set in on Halloween, I mean I´m probably gonna play this a couple of days after it, but hey as long as it still in Autumm that is good enough for me.

## To Play

Because chances are I´m gonna play these way outside of the spooky season, but they caught my attention when I looking for stuff to play this month. Here are some honorable mentions:


- [**Look Outside**](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3373660/Look_Outside/): A creepy turn-based survival horror RPG, a mysterious event that mutates anyone who looks outside into grostesque monsters, forces our main character be locked up up in his apartament, eventually he is led to explore outside to find supplies and figure out what is causing all of this.
- [**Crow Country**](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1996010/Crow_Country/): A survivor horror game with playstation style graphics, where you investigate an abandoned theme park. I normally dont do survivor horror, but the game comes with an Exploration mode that removes enemies and allows you to focus on puzzle solving and the horror atmosphere, which I wish more horror games had more of.
- [**Night Loops**](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1826060/Night_Loops/): Pixaleted horror adventure, taking inspiration from Yumie Nikki and Twins Peaks, there´s some puzzle solving, multiple Endings and each day is resetted back into a time loop.
- [**Gabriel Night Sins of the Father**](https://www.gog.com/en/game/gabriel_knight_sins_of_the_fathers): This one is a classic from the golden age of adventure puzzle games. I had plans for this one because of the late Tim Curry´s acting in this. That being said I prefer the original version versus its rerelease.
- [**The Excavation of Hobs Barrow**](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1182310/The_Excavation_of_Hobs_Barrow/): This last one is another for the puzzle adventure crowd. Folk horror with the protagonists being a writer doing a books on English Barrows, seems like it has has a decent creepy atmosphere.
- [**NightHawks**](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1221950/Nighthawks/): Wadget Games keeps on Delivering Lookie here, yet another RPG Vampire game. But text-based mostly with voice acting. Vampires are public knowledge, and as a newly turned creature of the night you get to run a night club. Greate if you´re into reading game dialogue a lot.
- [**Mouthwashing**](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2475490/Mouthwashing/): I assume this one needs to introduction, but if you have been living under a rock, it´s about a space crew of five people stranded in space, and then all hell breaks lose as you get to see the crew lose themselves to stravation and madness
