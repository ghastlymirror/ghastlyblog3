## How Did I Get Here

On a previous post I mentioned that I was playing WOW on a private server, I also made a small mention about playing on the now defunct Nostrum.

During its short run (it only lasted a month), it was a progressive Wrath of The Lich KIng Server, where progress was divided by phases, for phase one everyone started at the same level and it would be capped at level 20, then second phase would happens, more dungeons would be unlocked and you could level until 30.

The idea was to let people have a similiar experience back when WOW was released where everyone got to experience all the expansions as they opened up up to WOW  at the same time.

And it worked, the server started making waves they even made a special ingame event involving a world boss, and then more people started joining in the second phase... until it got shutdown.

The owners of the server shared the news about it doing to Blizzard and that was the end of that, now I´m not much for speculation, but I´ve heard that it wasn´t blizzard this time and that some other issue.

Some of us decided to then either take a break from WOW or move to somewhere else, maybe to Chromiecraft which was  another progressive server that Nostrum based itself on, but it has already progressed to Wrath of The Lich King.

And Thus it Ended the Adventures of Freyna My Dwarf Hunter...

![A red haired female dwarf riding a top of a giant ram](/images/2026/freynaride.jpg)

Or Did it?

Turns some members of a guild there (called Agressive Others), decided to take manners into their own hands and make their own version called Project Alternac, but with some extra features like customized content. It has just opened up its second phase (up to level 30)

I was pondering, should or should not join in? For one its doesnt have a lot of population (although the tier and help consolidate everyone in the same leveling zone) and I would probabbly be starting behind those who already been there from the start, so I can´t really expect be easily find people to party with as I level like what happened in Nostrum.

What the heck, I´m bored and the higher XP for lower levels players might help me get there faster.

So here´s what I´m do I´m gonna recreated my defunct character, speedrun the whole thing till I reach the level I was before the shutdown (level 27 I think) and I´m gonna document it. So join me on my adventures. 

![WOW Character creation Screen](/images/2026/freynacreation.jpg "It Begins")

And hey if anyone reading wants to jumpin [and make an account](https://alterac.gg/), the more the merrier, don´t be afraid to add me and send me a message.

## First Steps

Normally I prefer to play male characters, but the idea of playing a badass female dwarf appeals to me so I decided to create Freyna instead of a whole new character.

As I always, I started on Coldridge Valley, same old same old. Killed some wolves, Troggs and delivered a dwarf his mead, and by then I leveled quickly to 6 or seven.

![A female red haired dwarf sitting in the snow](/images/2026/freynasitting.jpg)

When I was done with it I moved up the tunnels to the rest of Dun Morough and reach Kharanos, got my first Hearthstone location.

I picked up some professions as well, minining and engineering as must have for hunters since it will allow me to save up on bullets. It was around this area where I got into my first hunting quest and was able to get my very first pet a bear (I haven´t decided what name to give it suggestions are open).

From there it was smooth sailing. See I picked the bear because they are execellent tanks, which I will be needing since I have yet to see a single soul around here. I guess talking to people in Global chat does offset the loneliness a bit, but I might find more players as I progress.

And with my Guard Bear in tow I am now on Level 12 trying to explore a Troll cave.

![Freyna the Dwarf In a Ince Cave With A bear next to her](/images/2026/freynacave.jpg)

There are some fun quests in this tough, I delivered a group of Dwarves their bag of ammo which they proceeded to start shooting practice. One dwarf turn out to have really terrible aim much to the mockery of his colleagues.

And then another pair of Dwarves (the smallest of two if not the all groups is called Shorty much to my amusement), used a canon and let it rip. I swear I heard one of them yell Mortal Kombat.

![A group of several Dwarves, the shortest one is standing at the top of a stool](/images/2026/fryenashorty.jpg)

The other funny quest was where I had to deliver Alchohol (again) but this time I had to the Inn, bribe a dwarf with mead, and then steal part of the barrel while he´s distracted. Dwarves sure love their mead.

All this in the background I learn that most dwarves went off to war, leaving the small folk to defend the area and something terrible has happened to the city Gnomeregan, leading dozens of gnomes to become refugees.

But that is a story for another entry.
