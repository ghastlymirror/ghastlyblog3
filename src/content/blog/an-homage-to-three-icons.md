Like many of you I recently heard of the passing of three famous people, Dolly Parton, Tim Curry and Peter Cullen, it was sad but to be expected considering their age.

But as one Dr. Seuss quote says, don´t cry because its over, smile because it happened. These three have touched and inpsired others in so many ways, so here´s to them. 

![Dolly Partron](/images/blog/dollypartron.jpg)

Dolly for example not only was a talented performer (but to be fair I mostly know her for the hit song Jolene and [9 to 5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UbxUSsFXYo4)), who only gave her contributions to country music, but to education, charity and from what I heard [science](https://www.zmescience.com/feature-post/pieces/dolly-parton-science-tribute/). And she showed solidarity with the LGBT+ community, specially since nowadays that seems like its in short supply.


Then there´s our dearly departed Tim, to me he was practically inescapable in my childhood, if there was a [cartoon ](https://www.timcurry.co.uk/voice-over-cartoons)chances are he was in it. While people rember him as the Dad from the [Wild Thornberries](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ooPIjm-8PtY) I remember him as Trader Slick from [Jumanji](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8jtCQGfOiDM).

And considering I talk about videogames a lot, I cannot mention his foray in this area as Gabriel Knight, the infamous [Space Communist](https://youtu.be/g1Sq1Nr58hM) from Red Alert 3 and also Brutal Legend. But what he was most known for is his movie roles.

![Tim Curry as A Butler](/images/2026/timcurryhomealone.jpg)

It would be hard to just showcase all of them, but my top favorites besides him playing the role of a [sweet transvestite](https://youtu.be/JWoYy4Ah81s) from transexual transylvania, were [Lord Darkness](https://youtu.be/Ii-X-6UbIvU) from Legend, The Butler From [Clue](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=srmUxlOMD_c), and when he played as a muppet.

You could say his wild performances were inspir for ationthose outcasts and weirdos out there, I still find myself quoting "Don´t Dream it be it" from time to time.

But the universe decided to be even extra ironic and have this two left us in the same day at the same age.

![Peter Cullen](/images/2026/petercullen.jpg)

And then there´s Peter Cullen, who also died around the same time spane but 5 years old, most of yall knew as [Optimus Prime](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tFex4y45BNU) but I knew him as [Eyeore](https://youtu.be/z0-JzoSU1Zo) from Winnie the Pooh, I rememeber hearinng someone say, that his versions talks as disgruntled army vet and I´m living for it. He was also in Invicible? Wait, is that why I saw a lot of Transformers memes in that fandom?


I´m gonna pour one out for them they will be missed.
