I was supposed to regale yall with the continuation of my Warcraft adventures on the Project Alterac but sadly a couple of week ago the owner hits us with the news that they no longer are able to maintain the server due to to cost and also lack of time. I´m not gonna boggle down on the details or wonder much why they couldn´t just pass the project to a new set of hands, since they others GMs had shown interested in keeping it alive...

So the whole community got together for one last hurrah of epic proportions and I got the pics to prove it.

## Goodbye Alterac

Despite having a lower population, it made up for it for having a social and tightknit community, there was a bit of comfort in watching familiar names show up on chat and running around. Something that you don´t just get much on more populated server or even the official ones anymore.

Some of them were chaotic or a bit edgy but it was all in good fun, the most fun I had in World of Warcfraft in ages, my favorite memory will be when we had to escort our low level DJ to Blackrock to host a dance session there. People would go out they´re way to make their own events such as renancting Frodo´s walk to Mordor by having a bunch of players acompanying a level 1 gnome (which ended in him falling into lava).

Alas, all good things must comes to an end, such is the risk in playing in a private server (or even in official mmos), you don´t know how long it mgiht lat so you try to enjoy it while you can.

What was the saying again? Don´t cry because it´s over. Smile because it happened.

I don´t know where I go after, this as of now I´m now hanging on Chromiecraft as the Undead Liamannis or the newly release server Whitemane Frostmourne recreating my dwarf Freyna, or as Jareth the Elf.

AlthoguhtI´m still on the fence wether or not I will play WOW Forever, we´ll see how the release goes.

Farewell Alterac and thanks for all the fish.
