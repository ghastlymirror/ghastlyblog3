Today is the last day of August and with it the end of this event, As well summer vacation or the start at one as I know someone people who only take vacations in Setember. Can´t say I blame them, have you´ve seen the excess of tourism in August lately?

But I digress it´s time for me to see how well I did. And looking at my archive I actually did way better than last, counting with this one I posted about 15 times. I gotta say it wasn´t easy trying to come up with topics in such short notice, I guess certain events like the Solar eclipse and my frog tank situation helped a little.

I wish I could´ve written more, but as I´ve talked before on this site, I had to move into a new place, get it to be how I liked it and between fixing the kitchen, painting the walls, weeding the garden, helping the family, It wasn´t easy trying to come up with the time to write.

But write I did, and had fun doing it, wether this means my writting has improved your mileage may vary.

I also decided to something a bit different this year, with the sheer amount of new people and the whole section of the blaugust community deciding to do groups projects I decided to make my own. At the end of every post I would share links of articles written by fellow participants that caught my attention, because this isn´t just about me but the blogosphere as a whole.

It also gave be the oportunity to find new blogs to my blogroll, I realized I haven´t updated it in a while.

Still feels a bit sad thought, maybe it´s just me but this event makes the blogsphere feel more alive and dynamic to me, it´s also the time of the year that forces me to pay a bit more attention to my site site, instead of neglecting it and post every two weeks and its usally my old wordpress posts I´ve been transferring.

Pretty sure I´m gonna be taking a big break from blogging in September, specially as autum will be arriving and I gotta make even more preparations for my garden. Funny enough maybe I might even put a garden section on this site using it as inspiration for future articles, even though the main focus as been mostly gaming, with a bit of photography and other insterests sprinkled in.

Speaking of gaming I feel like I have yet to write a review of two adventure games that I finish back in May, mostly I´ve been procrastinating. But yeah keep an eye out for those two. And hopefully I´m this close to finish putting the rest of the post from my old site, leaving me to finnaly focus on writting new stuff.

That´s it for this month. Anyway, It was nice meeting you all and having some fun discussions, I hope we keep doing it the near future.

Happy End of Blaugust!
