Remember when [earlier this month](/blog/what-i-am-playing-this-summer-2026) I talked about the frog living in my watering tank? Well, some new developments. So, anyway I had open up the tank to drain it a little bit and mind (its fine it never empties it completely) and it formed a little pond/creek

![A small creek in the middle of grass](/images/2026/frogcreek.jpg#medium)

And guess who moved right in. Yeah that´s right the tiny newly developed frogs have escaped containment and are now living here I was wondering why I didn´t seemed to see a lot of them in tank lately, so this where they´ve been the whole time.

![A close up of a very small frog](/images/2026/froggytiny.jpg#center)

![A close up of a small frog](/images/2026/secondsmallfrog.jpg#center)


And man, looking them up up close really makes me realise how really tiny they are, I had to try to get close and use zoom just capture them on my phone. I curse myself for not bringing my camera with the micro lens with me, but slowy moving here and get the house ready has been so chaotic that it has been low priority

![A close up of a small frog](/images/2026/thirdtinyfrog.jpg#center)

![A close up of a small frog](/images/2026/fourthfrog.jpg#center)


I guess that solves sorta my conudrum of how to clean the tank while making sure the frogs have a decent place to live. That being now there is a new issue, if I were to close the drain competely and in this heat the small artifical greek might dry up completely. You know I´ve been eyeing this small artifical pond container while I was shopping on the hardware store for some gardening supplies.

Maybe I could get it and make a small space for them. It would be nice to maintain a certain frog population in the garden, the do make for decent pest control you know.
