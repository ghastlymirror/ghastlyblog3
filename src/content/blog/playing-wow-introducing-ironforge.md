When [I last left off](/blog/starting-over-at-a-new-wow-server-project-alternac), I was doing some quests in a Troll Cave and tamed a bear. Now I progressed further through Dun Morough, my next quest sent me towards no other that Ironforge, the Dwarve´s Capital City.

An ancient place full of history and interesting Lore, with an impressive entrance.

![A large stone entrance to the city](/images/blog/entrancetoironforge.jpg)

![A giant statue of dwarf lifting a hammer and an axe above his head](/images/2026/inforgestatue.jpg)


But My favorite part has to be The explorer´s guild, that place is pratically museum so I go to enjoy some the exibits and read some of the library books. Did you know that Ecologists are actually a thing in WOW? Imagine a whole field of study dedicated to study like Dragons or the many spiders you kill for quests? What is the ecological niche of Murlocs I wonder?


![A giant dragon skull exibit](/images/2026/dragonskull.jpg)

![A giant skeleton of a ram](/images/2026/ramskeleton.jpg)


After my tour to the city, visiting the bank and action house it was time to do some more quests which led me to the main court Hall where I met the local ruler and Senator Barin Redstone who doesn´t seem to like me very much in fact he doesn´t like any of us low pleasants, and deliver him my report.


![A red haired and bearded dwarf wearing a blue robe](/images/2026/dwarfsenator.jpg)


Afterwards A Gnome asked me for help to find his missing Robot Bingles, and I finally found an actual player! They didn´t seem to be all that talkative.

![A small gnome wearing glasses asking me to find his robot](/images/2026/findtherobot.jpg)

![Antoher Player character iwth the name of Oof from the Avarice Quild, she looks like a blue woman with ram horns, a tail and goat legs](/images/2026/anotherplayerinironforge.jpg)


Once all of that was done, I killed some more Troggs that were infesting A mining, which then lead me to the farthest area to deliver YET another batch of Ale (Dwarves sure love their Ale) and I was asked to find a missing Dwarf.

Didn´t take me long to find him, well he´s a corpse. Gang, looks like we have a mystery on our hands!


![The corpse of a dead dwarf in the snow](/images/2026/deaddwarffound.jpg)


Turns out the culprit was Huge White Bear so me and my pet, posed for a photo for posterity, I mean its practically a Hunter tradition.


![A female red haired dwarf standing next to a dead white bear and a living brown bear](/images/2026/polarbeartrophy.jpg)


This leaves me now at level 13 and with Dun Morough fully explored, onwards to Loch Modan!


![Achivement Earned Explore Dun Morough](/images/2026/dummoroughcompleted.jpg)
