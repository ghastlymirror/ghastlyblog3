![A shelf full of Books](/images/blog/bookshelf.jpg)

Recently, I decided to get back into collecting physical books again, I realized that I actually enjoy reading them at the beach instead looking at a screen, and I´ve already read the books I´ve already own  for years so I´d figure why not get some new additions.

Plus the Book fair is in town which happens during this time of year. However, as I hover over the booths, and try to read the summaries I feel like nothing there speaks to me. I dont why or what happened, did I lost that spark for reading?

Used to be I was a bit more adventurous where I would take a chance on some author I never heard off, go by the summary alone and hope for the best it´s a decent book. But now?

It could be what happened is as I´ve gotten older my tastes have mtured and I know what I like, that I´ve become more conscious of my own expenses, and thus be more selective where my money goes to.

I feel like the oposite of those BookTok users who make videos who share their hauls of books, and its not just the money, there´s limited space in my bookshelf, only the worthy may enter my shelf of honor.

Or maybe I found the selection actually lacking, there was one less booth in previous years, a lot of it is a selection of mostly crime novels and romance, same old fantasy bestsellers.

There were a few gems though, one booth was selling manga, and I really wanted to buy that first  (and only) volume of the Summer that Hikaru Died, but sadly the seller was not around and I wasn´t going to wait for them to show up, I guess I picked the wrong hour.

At this point I kinda gave up and settled for one of the Fighting Fantasy Gamebooks instead. Yeah, it´s more of a game than a story you could just passively read at your leisure, but I´ve been trying to get into Solo TTRPGs for a while and this seemed like a good place to start.

![A fighting Fantasy book](/images/2026/fightingfantasy.jpg#medium)

And there was not a single book from the Witcher series! Seriously you would think with the netflix and the popularity of the games, there would be more of it available for sale, since the same thing happened George R. Martin´s series. I guess this must be a bit of a fluke, because I was lucky to find the first two at the supermarket at a discount, and then I never found them again.

I guess my best bet would be to go to Fnac where they have a much more diverse selection plus they also have some available in a Foreign Language (namely English), which is great since I prefer to try to read in their original form whenever I can.

My other gripes seem is that pocket sized even paperbacks seem harder to come by, something that could fit in my small bag (I guess now I know why those big book bags became trendy). Portugal doesnt seem to have that of a big pocket book tradition vs the Anglosphere.

Just for comparison I have the english version of A Song of Ice of Fire Books and when compared to the portuguese translation, the difference is a night and day. I also got the portuguese version of The Godfather also pocket sized. But that size is in the minority (unless its a book for a young audience) .

It´s at this point I´m reminded of why I started to flock to digital in the first place. I´m telling you the state of the Portuguese Book Industry feels abysmal and limited. And with the books you really want can only be  easily found online and dealing with shipping fees...

So yeah, ebooks are currently the solution to maintaining my interest in literature, even though they are not as collectible. And I might consider getting a library card for the more physical experience without blowing my budget (and bookself space). And to avoid getting ripped off cuz to hell with Kindle and Amazon, I´m only getting it from places that sell DRM-Free Ones, like [ebook.com](https://www.ebooks.com/) or [bookshop.org](https://bookshop.org/shop/thebookdragon).
 
 Then I would download all that on my phone and app reader of choice (I hear [Moonreader](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flyersoft.moonreader&hl=en-US) is great), also some friend of mine recomended the [Hardcover.app](https://hardcover.app/), where not only is it useful to track your reads but people can share their recs, might be a good place to find what I´m really interested in.

 That being said, I´m not giving up on getting physical copies completely (maybe I might just buy a book ot ywo a year), specially if I find a title that really really catches my eye. But guys, it´s hard out there for a Portuguese Bookworm.
