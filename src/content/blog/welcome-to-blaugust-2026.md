Yes, August is finally here and with it starts my participation on this year´s Blaugust event. Now I`ve alright talked about it entails, so I´ll refer to [my previous post](/blog/nlauguust-2026-is-almost-here) if you haven´t read it already.

Normally, whenever I start this event I usually make some small reflection about what do I want out of this and how am i going about this..

And my answer to that? Probably bond with fellow bloggers and maybe get some extra eyes on my blog, which is usually more around this time of the year, perhaps I am more active and people prefer blog who are always updating at a frequent pace.

Of course I will cheese it and just write usually during the same day, never been much for planning. That being said it is easier said than done, As I´ve been lately been very, very busy this past summer, so i´ll try to do my best.

So, why am I was this busy? Well, I´ve some fun news, I´m gonna be moving to a new place eventually! Yes after ages and ages of house hunting (By God is the Portuguese Housing Market bleak), I found a nice little corner of my own, it even comes with a garden. And yes my dream of having my own vegetable garden, is finally coming true!

The possibilities are endless, heck I might even raise some egg laying chickens (I Have the space for it), and who knows, maybe I might share my progress on here.

However, I´m not ready to move there yet, there´s a lot to do before its considered livable up to my taste,  paint some walls, move some of my personal stuff, weed the garden, fix some stuff...

It´s a brand new chapter in life, it will not be easy leaving the place I´ve always known, but the neighbours seem nice. Also shout out to my neighbour who gifted me a bunch of extra peaches she caught from her relatives farm, she´s a gem.

Life updates aside, due the fact that I´ve gonna be doing a lot of back and forth, it´s not got be suprsing if I fail a day or two, I´ve made my peace long ago that I´ve never finish the 31 days...

Here´s what I´m gonna do, in the days am I able to write I´ll try to focus on chosing a theme, it could be what media I´be been into, summer recomendations, or heck post that I´ve been the backburner that I´ve gotten to writting yet.

Also, at the end of each post I might share a link to posts from other fellow participants that caught my attention, because this events is also a great way to find some new bloggers and who knows, maybe they might even end on my blogrolls, plus I like to give a little leg up for beginners. 

This concludes the end of this post see, Happy Blaugust and i´ll yall in the next one.
