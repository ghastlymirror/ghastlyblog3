## WarioWare Twisted

![WarioWare Twisteed Cover](/images/blog/wariowaretwsited.png#center)

A very oddball of a game, but what warioware titles is it. A series of fun and little minigames that you gotta complete before the titles ends, testing out your gaming reflexes and following the misadventures of Wario and his friends.

This Game Boy Advance title, plays a bit differently as it made use of the cartridge´s built-in gyro sensor and rumble feature, where you physically have to move the handheld. As well even if you fail a minigame you can still reach the final boss, which makes this game a bit easier.

I´ve been playing this one my phone using the [linkyboy emulator](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pixelrespawn.linkboy&hl=pt) that does allow to emulated those function by using the device´s on gyro, and I´ve been having a blast, and if you add retroachivements to the mix, it helps create more challenges.

Though, I only did this because on the 3ds this feature is only available on mgba emulator, but since it only runs in the new 3DS (mine is an older model), I had to make do.

Not to despair, as there is a [no gyro mod](https://romhackplaza.org/romhacks/warioware-twisted-improved-no-tilt-game-boy-advance/)

## Eterspire

![A player from eterspire game wearing purple armor on a horse](/images/2026/eterspirescreen.png#center)

A simple but cozy oldschool indie mmorpg, been playing this one a lot on my phone. It doenst require much of me if I got a few moments to spare. Even thought it has a simple story one, that sometimes is enough for me. It has some cutesy grpahics and funny dialogue that parodies your typical fantasy mmo tropes.

You can progress throught the game by doing main quests that progress through the storyline, sidequests which allow you to epxlore the world fruther, and hunting quest where you hunt a certain numbers mosnter. Pro tip, don´t neglet your hunting quests as they help you progress faster and some areas in the game are closed off until you reach a certain level.

That being said there is no pay to win, as it only requires your skill and dedication. You can buy some cosmetics, but usually goes to support the game, and if you pay a onetime fee you can acess the rest of the content, like extra quests and speciall currency, among other extra features but there is still plently to enjoy as free player.

Might consider buying this one if I get really invested in this one to support the creators. And its has crossplay, so you can play on your pohne and then continue your progress once you return home to your pc.

I was also told it´s a good idea to join their [discord server](https://discord.com/servers/eterspire-814967791840264232) as they are always having community made events and contest which provide with fun ingame prizes.

## Pokemon Ranger

![Pokemon Ranger Game Cover](/images/2026/pokemonranger.webp#medium)

In this Pokemon spinoff, you play as a Pokemon Ranger. And instead of adquiring a team roster of mons to train and beat gym leaders to become the very best, you spend most of you´re time helping humans and pokemon alike. The region of Fiore is an unique reason in which people and pokemon coexist peacefully and trainer are seen as novelty that only exist outside of the island.

As a new recruit you start off small like helping capture pokemon to dispell conflict or recruit said pokemon to help with situations like putting out forests fires, that sort of thing. But in the middle of this there this nefarious organixation who has decided to steal the rangers new capture technology for neafrious means and its up to you and your felow rangers to stop them.

Gameplay is very different from your main line games since it makes use of the DS stylus. In order to capture a pokmeon you gotta draw circles around a pokemon a certain number of times. If a pokemon goes agianst the line whilc capture is still in progress it breaks, and if it attacks your line it also deplites your device´s baterry.

There is also a bit of a strategy in this, as captured Pokemon can temporary join your roster and you can use their special abilities to help slow down and even paralyse said pokemon making the process easy. They can also be used in missions, for example in order to help a young woman lift the bo at the entranc eof her own , I had to find and recruit a fighting pokemon.

That being unlike other games,  not only will pokemon of your team lave once you use their special ability, but also when you leave and are due to the games in explanation about rangers not being allowed to remove pokemon out of their habitats.

I found the concept of Pokemon rangers fascinating and its a shame that the series remains stuck in the ds and no further sequels have been made. That being said I wish they did allow you to choose a Pokemon Partner, minun is cute and all but it my least favorite.

## Last Day of June

![Last Day of June Cover](/images/2026/lastdayofjune.png#center)

Despite the title, its the name of person and not the end of the month since I innitially thought since I was playing this one blind. Its been in my backlog for a while thanks to the weekly epic games giveaway, so why not give it a shot?

It´s a melancholic third person puzzle of game about a man who lost his beloved wife in a car accident that left him on a wheelchair, and through the use of special paintings he is able to turn back time and try to change the events that led to this.

There are four different character you take control off, each chapter starts off with the player reenacting the original eventones than afterwards you gotta figure out how to change them. After each chapter ends you get to see how it alters the course.

However, chancing one event isn´t easy. For example I will stop the kid from playing on the road but then another character ends up involved in the accident. And sometimes in order to finish a new chapter I gotta go back and reset the previous characte event to its orginial form. And back and forth.

Fortunately the game doesnt force me to go through, the steps to reenact them, you get a simple command of ending the day. Other than that I haven´t gone far enough into the game to form a more detailed opinion, perhaps on a future review.

Looking at the style, I dunno kinda reminds me of those short indie animations with a bit of slice of life and dealing with the death of love ones. Your mileage will vary if you like the eyeless dolls look. But its not bad for a game made by an Italian studio.

Did I forget to mention it was based on the song [Drive Home](Drive Homehttps://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7ce1XG4RhwU) by Steven Wilson?
